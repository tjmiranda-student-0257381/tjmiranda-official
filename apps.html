Good to Know

How these apps handle your information

  • Geerho Beta Testing: Geerho is distributed safely through Apple TestFlight. Founding Seller status is linked to your initial beta registration, granting you lifetime 0% commission and 1 item daily (30 listings/month).
  • Everything stays on your device (for local tools). Health Tracker entries are saved in your own browser storage. I never receive them, and there is no server holding your data.
  • No account, no sign-in for browser tools. Nothing to register for and no password to forget for our standalone client-side utilities.
  • You own the backups. Download a backup file whenever you like, keep it wherever you like, and restore it on any device.
  • Clearing your browser clears local app data. Wiping browsing data, switching browsers, or moving to a new phone leaves the data behind — that is what backups are for.
  • Shared devices need care. Anyone who uses the same browser profile on that device can open local tools and see what is saved.

Health Tracker is for personal record keeping only. It is not medical advice and must never be the basis for a life-and-death decision. Always consult your doctor.
