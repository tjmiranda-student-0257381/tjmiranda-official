Good to Know

How these apps handle your information

  • Everything stays on your device. Your entries are saved in your own browser storage. I never receive them, and there is no server holding your data.
  • No account, no sign-in. Nothing to register for and no password to forget.
  • You own the backups. Download a backup file whenever you like, keep it wherever you like, and restore it on any device.
  • Clearing your browser clears the app. Wiping browsing data, switching browsers, or moving to a new phone leaves the data behind — that is what backups are for.
  • Shared devices need care. Anyone who uses the same browser profile on that device can open the app and see what is saved.

Health Tracker is for personal record keeping only. It is not medical advice and must never be the basis for a life-and-death decision. Always consult your doctor.
