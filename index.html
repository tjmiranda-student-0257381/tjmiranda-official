Welcome

Hello, I’m TJ Miranda

I’m a U.S. Army Veteran, U.S. Navy spouse, and dedicated public servant. I served the U.S. Navy as a federal civilian Utility Systems Operator (Water & Wastewater Treatment Specialist), and my career has spanned military service, public utilities, leadership, and hands-on technical work.

I hold a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and am back in college to refresh and sharpen my core technical skills. Long before modern web development, I was deeply immersed in tech as one of only two hackers in Oriental Mindoro during the 1990s. Beyond my technical background, I have spent years serving my local community in Kings County.
